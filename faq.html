About the store

What is Arun Karyana Store?

A family-run kirana on Railway Road, Barara, serving the town since 1977 — atta, rice, dal, masale, chai, snacks, dairy, household and personal care, now available to order online.

Where are you?

Railway Road, Barara, Ambala, Haryana 133201. Drop by any time we’re open — the counter is still the heart of the shop.

When are you open?

Every day. Monday–Friday 8 AM – 9 PM, Saturday 8 AM – 10 PM, Sunday 9 AM – 8 PM. You can order online at any hour.

Ordering & delivery

How do I order?

Add what you need to your bag, tap Checkout, enter your name, phone and address, and place the order. We’ll call to confirm. You can also simply WhatsApp us your list.

Is there a minimum order?

No. Orders of ₹500 or more get free delivery; below that there’s a ₹40 delivery charge.

How quickly will it arrive?

Usually within 24–48 hours, and often the same day for orders placed before noon (subject to availability).

Can I track my order?

Yes — sign in and open My orders to see where each order is. Or call or WhatsApp us for a quick update.

Payment & prices

How do I pay?

You pay when your order arrives — cash or UPI.

Do you have offers?

Yes. Current offers appear on the homepage; promo codes go in your bag, and automatic offers apply themselves.

Are online prices the same as in the shop?

Yes — the same fair price whether you shop online or at the counter.

Your account

Do I need an account to order?

No — you can order as a guest. An account lets you save addresses, check out faster and follow your orders.

How do I create one?

Tap the person icon at the top, choose Create account, and enter your name, email, mobile and a password.

I forgot my password.

Use Forgot password on the sign-in page. If the email doesn’t arrive, WhatsApp us and we’ll set a new password for you within minutes.

Returns & cancellations

Can I cancel an order?

Yes, until it leaves the store. Call +91 94168 91710 or WhatsApp us as soon as you can.

What if something is damaged or wrong?

Tell us within 24 hours of delivery and we’ll replace it or refund you. See our return policy for details.

Still have a question? Call +91 94168 91710 or WhatsApp us — a real person at the counter will answer.
