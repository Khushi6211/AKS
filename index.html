04 Our story

Railway Road, Barara · अरुण करियाना

One counter. Four decades of Barara.

1977

The counter opens.

A small kirana opens on Railway Road with a brass tarazu, a ledger and one rule that has never changed: every customer takes home exactly what they pay for.

1990s

A town grows up with us.

Children who came for toffees come back for their own family’s monthly ration. The ledger fills with familiar names.

2010s

The next generation.

The shelves get fuller and the brands more varied — the welcome, and the weighing, stay exactly the same.

Today

Barara’s pantry, online.

Browse the shelves from your phone, order in a minute and pay when it arrives. The counter is still there whenever you’d rather drop by.
