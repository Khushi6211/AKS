Our story

Four decades of weighing things fairly.

In 1977, Arun Kumar opened a small kirana on Railway Road with a brass scale, a ledger and one simple promise: every customer takes home exactly what they pay for.

Generations of Barara families have shopped at this counter since. The shelves are fuller now and orders arrive on the phone too — but the welcome, and the promise, are the same.
