01 — THE COUNTER

1977EST.

Before the supermarkets. Before the apps. There was a counter on Railway Road. In 1977, Arun Kumar opened it with a brass scale, a ledger, and one simple promise: every customer takes home exactly what they pay for. The scale has been polished a thousand times since. The promise hasn't changed.

02 — THE PROMISE

Three things we've never cut corners on.

Four decades in, the store has changed shape — the shelves are fuller, the orders now come by phone and screen — but the way we work is still the way it began.

i.

Fair measure

Every kilo is a full kilo. Goods are weighed and packed with the same care you'd get standing at the counter.

ii.

Honest prices

Fair, competitive prices on the brands your family already trusts — with regular offers for loyal customers.

iii.

To your doorstep

Order in a minute, pay in cash or UPI when it arrives. Delivery across Barara is free on orders over ₹500.
