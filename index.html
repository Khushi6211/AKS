Our story

Four decades of trust, one neighbourhood.

Established in 1977 by Arun Kumar, Arun Karyana Store has served the Barara community with quality household products at honest prices.

Our mission hasn't changed: a wide range of daily essentials, the highest standards of quality, and service that feels like family — now with the convenience of ordering online.
