Returns & refunds.

If something isn’t right, tell us within 24 hours and we’ll make it right.

Last updated: January 2025

What can be returned

We accept returns when:

  • A product arrives damaged or defective
  • You received a different item from the one you ordered
  • A product is expired or close to expiry
  • Something is missing from your order

Please tell us within 24 hours of delivery.

What can’t be returned

For health and safety reasons we can’t take back:

  • Opened food items or beverages
  • Used personal care products
  • Products with tampered packaging
  • Items without their original packaging

How to return

1 · Contact us

Call +91 94168 91710 or WhatsApp us within 24 hours of delivery.

2 · Share the details

Your order number, what went wrong, and a photo of the item.

3 · We collect it

We pick the item up from your door at no extra cost.

4 · Replacement or refund

We bring the correct item, or refund you within 3–5 business days.

Refunds

  • Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded in cash or with a replacement product
  • Refunds are usually completed within 3–5 business days
  • We’ll confirm with you once your refund is done
