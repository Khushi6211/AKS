Delivery areas

We currently deliver to:

  • Barara, Ambala, Haryana
  • Surrounding localities within a 10 km radius of the store

Not sure whether we reach you? Call us on +91 94168 91710 before you order.

Delivery charges

Delivery is free on orders of ₹500 and above. Orders below ₹500 have a ₹40 delivery charge. The exact charge is always shown in your bag before you place the order.

Delivery time

  • Standard delivery: within 24–48 hours of your order
  • Same-day delivery: for orders placed before 12:00 PM, subject to availability
  • Urgent requirements: call us and we’ll do our best

Deliveries can take a little longer during festivals, peak seasons or bad weather — we’ll keep you posted.

How your order moves

Placed

We receive your order and call you to confirm it.

Packed

We pick and pack your items at the counter, checking quality and weight.

On the way

Your order leaves the store for your door.

Delivered

Pay on delivery — cash or UPI.

Tracking your order

Signed-in customers can follow every order under My orders. You can also call or WhatsApp us any time for an update.

Delivery problems

  • Damaged items: take a photo and tell us within 24 hours — we’ll replace them
  • Wrong items: we’ll collect them and bring the right ones at no extra cost
  • Missing items: tell us within 24 hours and we’ll sort it out
  • Delays: we’ll keep you informed and prioritise your order

Orders we can’t deliver

An order may not reach you if the address is incomplete, no one is available after repeated attempts, we can’t reach you by phone, or the location is outside our delivery area. We’ll call you to arrange a new time or cancel, as you prefer.

Questions? Call +91 94168 91710, WhatsApp us or write to contact@arunkaryanastore.com.
