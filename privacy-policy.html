Your privacy, respected.

What we collect, why we collect it, and how we look after it.

Last updated: January 2025

What we collect

  • Personal details: name, email, phone number and delivery address
  • Order details: what you bought, when, and where it was delivered
  • Device details: IP address, browser and device type
  • Usage: pages visited and products viewed

How we use it

  • To process and deliver your orders
  • To confirm orders and send status updates
  • To answer your questions and support requests
  • To improve the store and the website
  • To send offers — only with your consent
  • To prevent fraud and keep the site secure

Sharing

We never sell your personal information. We may share it only with:

  • Service providers that help us deliver orders or run the website
  • Authorities, when the law requires it
  • A new owner, if the business is ever transferred

Security

We take sensible measures to protect your information from unauthorised access or misuse. Passwords are stored in encrypted form. No method of sending data over the internet is completely secure, but we work to keep your data safe.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Show you the information we hold about you
  • Correct anything that’s wrong
  • Delete your data
  • Stop sending you marketing messages

Write to contact@arunkaryanastore.com and we’ll help.

Cookies & local storage

We store a few things in your browser — like your bag and whether you’re signed in — so the site works smoothly. You can clear them any time in your browser settings.

Children

Our services are not directed at anyone under 18, and we don’t knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy we’ll post the new version here with a new date.
